Our Core Educational Philosophy

In a financial landscape filled with misleading wealth promises and unverified claims, TradeWiz stands apart by focusing strictly on process, probability, and risk control. We do not provide hot tips or guaranteed returns.

Instead, we teach traders how to evaluate market structure, identify institutional liquidity sweeps, calculate precise position sizes in INR, and maintain long-term emotional discipline across Forex, NSE/BSE Equities, Precious Metals, and Crypto markets.

1. Technical Rigor

Master top-down technical analysis, supply/demand zones, and session timings tailored for Indian Standard Time (IST).

2. Strict Risk Management

Enforce capital preservation formulas where no single setup risks more than 1% to 2% of total account balance.

3. Transparent Community

Study historical market behaviors, journal trade outcomes, and learn collaboratively alongside thousands of Indian traders.

Multi-Market Coverage

Our educational curriculum covers four major global asset classes:

  • ⚡ Forex Trading: Major & minor pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, GBPJPY) with session timings and lot sizing principles.
  • 📈 Indian Equities (NSE/BSE): NIFTY 50 / BANK NIFTY market structure, sector rotations, and swing breakouts.
  • 🥇 Gold & Commodities: XAUUSD order flow analysis, US Dollar index (DXY) inverse correlations, and macro rate drivers.
  • 🌐 Cryptocurrency: Bitcoin (BTC) & Ethereum (ETH) market cycles, liquidity heatmaps, and risk allocation.
