Our Editorial Policy & Content Guidelines

How TradeWiz researches, writes, reviews, and maintains high-integrity educational financial content for Indian traders.

1. Commitment to Fact-Based Educational Analysis

Trading financial markets involves high risk under Your Money Your Life (YMYL) guidelines. Every guide, session breakdown, trade setup, and calculator published on TradeWiz must adhere to strict factual accuracy, mathematical precision, and objective technical analysis.

2. No Financial Advice or Wealth Promises

TradeWiz explicitly prohibits false claims, guaranteed returns, or get-rich-quick narratives. TradeWiz is an educational platform and is not registered with SEBI as an Investment Advisor. All market breakdowns demonstrate historical chart patterns, price action principles, and position sizing formulas for learning purposes only.

3. Author Expertise & Peer Review

Content published on TradeWiz is created and reviewed by experienced market practitioners who specialize in technical price action, order flow dynamics, and risk management. Technical formulas (such as position sizing, pip valuation, and margin metrics) are independently verified before release.

4. Regular Updates & Maintenance

Financial rules, market structures, exchange fees, and brokerage tax rates evolve over time. TradeWiz conducts routine content audits to ensure that educational guides, trading tools, and compliance disclosures reflect the latest market realities.

5. Transparency & Risk Warnings

Every educational page includes clear risk notices emphasizing that past performance is not indicative of future results, and that capital loss is a real possibility in trading. Members are always encouraged to trade demo accounts or risk only disposable capital.
