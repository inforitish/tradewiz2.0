Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
TradeWiz Community
Minimal Data Collection
TradeWiz respects your privacy and is committed to transparency about how we handle your information. Since we operate primarily via WhatsApp and Telegram, and do not run any login-based web or app system, we collect minimal or no personal data directly.
01Information We May Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. This may include:

  • Voluntary data such as your name, email address, or contact number if you reach out to us via email or a feedback form
  • Your publicly available Telegram or WhatsApp username if you participate in our community groups
  • Any information you choose to share when contacting our support team
02Information We Do Not Collect

We are committed to minimising data collection. We explicitly do not collect:

  • Sensitive personal information such as Aadhaar number, PAN card details, or passport information
  • Banking or financial account details of any kind
  • Passwords, PINs, or any authentication credentials
  • Precise location data or device identifiers
  • Browsing history or behavioral data via cookies or analytics tools

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics tools on our website to monitor user behaviour.

03How We Use Your Information

Any voluntary data you share with us is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your queries or support requests
  • To send you relevant updates about our community or services you have enquired about
  • To process your membership and provide community access
  • To communicate important platform announcements

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

04Third-Party Platforms

Our community communications occur primarily on WhatsApp and Telegram. Both platforms have their own privacy policies that govern how they collect and use data. We are not responsible for the data collected, stored, or processed by these third-party platforms.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of WhatsApp and Telegram directly to understand how your data is handled on those platforms.

05Data Retention

We retain voluntary communication records — such as emails or support conversations — only for as long as necessary to provide support or respond to your enquiries. Once the purpose for which the data was collected is fulfilled, we do not retain it for longer than required.

06Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect any information you share with us from unauthorised access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. Any updates will be communicated through our community channels. Continued use of our services after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
